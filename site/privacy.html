Privacy policy

Altera Roof Services Ltd. ("Altera", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and what we do with it. We follow British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

What this website collects

Nothing. This website has no forms, no accounts, no cookies, and no analytics or advertising trackers. Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and to keep the site running.

When you contact us

If you call or text us, we collect only what you choose to share. Usually that's your name, phone number, the property address, a description of the problem, and any photos you send.

How we use it

  • To respond to you and arrange a roof check.
  • To inspect, quote, and do the work you ask for.
  • To keep job records, including before and after photos of the work.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for unrelated marketing.

Job photos

We photograph our work before and after, sometimes from the air. If we share a photo of your roof on this site or on social media, we remove location data from the file and never publish your address. If you'd rather we didn't share photos of your property, tell us and we won't.

Who we share it with

Only people who need it to do the job, such as suppliers or subcontractors working on your roof, or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

As long as we need it for the work, warranty, and legal or tax records, then we securely delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent to how we use it (subject to legal limits). Call us at (604) 441-7876.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
